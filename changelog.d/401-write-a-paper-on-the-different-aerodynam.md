### Added

- `plot_station_loads!`, `plot_panel_polar!` and `plot_panel_pressure!` draw one
  wing section into a 2D Makie axis: a structural station's aero point loads, the
  polar a VSM panel flies, and the `Cp` pattern of an `AeroPressure` panel.
- `papers/aero_couplings/`: a paper comparing `AeroDirect`, `ContinuousAero`,
  `AeroPressure` and `AeroPressure` with live polars on the V3 kite, with the
  scripts that produce its results.
